## 1. The technique (as it stands)

`remotePairs` (group `wing`, **order 116, base cost 35, weight 350**, unscoped, `assumesUniqueness: false`) in `core/js/engine/tech-wing.js` (SHA-256 `B325DD0F...B617`, frozen before E and unchanged after it, `engine-frozen.txt`, `engine-after-e.txt`; `difficulty.js` `476273AA...663C`), weighted in `generator/js/engine/difficulty.js` (`WEIGHTS.remotePairs = 350`, between `twoStringKite` 340 and `simpleColouring` 360: registry 112 skyscraper, 114 twoStringKite, **116 remotePairs**, 120 simpleColouring; the Hard tier gains the group `wing`, every other rule unchanged). Longest engine line 110.

- **Proof.** Open cells with exactly the same two candidates {*X*, *Y*}; two of them that are real peers (`model.peers`: row, column, box, region, diagonal, window, overlapped box or **cage**, complete or not) must take different digits, so along a path of such cells the digits alternate. A pair of digits held by fewer than four such cells costs nothing. Each component is two-coloured by a breadth-first walk from its lowest cell; **a component with an odd cycle is skipped whole**, never in part. Two cells of opposite colours that are not peers are at least three edges apart (odd), hold *X* and *Y* one each, and both digits leave every open cell that sees both of them and still has one; a target is never a cell of the component. The search takes components by lowest cell, then the lower end, then the higher end, and returns the first pair that has a target, with all its targets.
- **Finding.** `{ technique: 'remotePairs', cells (one shortest path between the chosen ends, ascending), placements: [], eliminations (target cell, then digit, both digits where held), i18nKey: 'technique.remotePairs.step', i18nArgs: { digits (ascending), ends (the two labels, cell order), length (edges: 3, 5, 7, ...) } }`; the arrays are the call's own, the state is only read.
- **Work (R10).** One unit is one edge extension (a same-pair open peer found while a cell of a component is expanded, in the colouring and in the walk of the witness) or one tested pair of ends; **`SG.wingSearch.REMOTE_PAIRS_WORK_LIMIT = 50000`**, the seam `SG.wingSearch.remotePairs(model, state, ctx, { workLimit })`; local counter; the parent budget at entry and at most every 256 units; a spent limit is `null` with `capHit: 'work'` (a component is used only after its colouring is complete, so a lost deduction is never an invented one); a spent budget is nothing found; cancellation and other errors propagate; observer `{ id, work, limit, capHit, retained: null }`. The brief's vector costs 12 units (6 colouring, 1 pair, 5 witness).
- **Structural maximum** (`structural-max.txt`), units per call: 168 (4×4), 576 (6×6), **2,038** (Classic, Greater-Than, Wordoku **and a Killer board with no cages**), 2,052 (Jigsaw), 2,170 (Diagonal), 2,215 (Hyper), 4,968 and 12,128 (12 and 16 digits), 6,120 (Butterfly), 8,950 (Flower), 11,592 (Sohei), **16,384 (Samurai, 33 % of the limit)**. **Caged Killer** (`killer-bound.txt`/`.json`; the earlier census labelled "Killer" used a board with no cages): 2,268 for the 25-cage Killer puzzle of the help catalogue (52 units, 1,638 peers, nine cage-only peer edges), 2,274 for 27 box cages, **3,131 for any generated layout** (cages of 2 to 7 cells) and **7,505 for an imported layout of any shape**; the limit binds on no registered board.
- **Pattern cost** (`pattern-cost.txt`/`.json`, 4 cases × 20 runs, 80 finds, none capped, recorded again at order 116; the order-132 record is `pattern-cost-order132-superseded.*`): the two positives 12 and 26 units, the two negatives 4 and 0.
