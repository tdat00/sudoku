## 1. The technique (as it stands)

`finnedSwordfish` (group `fish`, order 134, base cost 48, weight 480, unscoped, `assumesUniqueness: false`) in `core/js/engine/tech-fish.js` (SHA-256 `798717B7...33C6`, frozen before E and unchanged at the end, `engine-frozen.txt`; `difficulty.js` `2C399660...FD9C`), weighted in `generator/js/engine/difficulty.js` (`WEIGHTS.finnedSwordfish = 480`, between `swordfish` 420 and `jellyfish` 520: registry order 130 swordfish, **134 finnedSwordfish**, 135 cageSplit, 137 jellyfish; the Hard tier, the Expert-and-above floor and every other rule unchanged). Longest engine line 110.

- **Proof.** Digit *d*; three base lines (complete rows, or columns, without a placed *d* and with at least two candidates) and three covers (complete lines of the other kind). A base candidate on a cover is a *body cell*, one on none a *fin*. Every base candidate keeps `findFish`'s ownership (its base line is the only line of its kind that holds it, it has exactly one cover line), there is a body cell in every base line and on every cover, at least one fin, and all fins lie in one single-owner box-like unit (`box` or `region`; an overlapped box of a multi-grid board gives nothing; a board without a complete box-like unit has no pattern and costs no work). Targets: open cover cells that can still be *d*, outside the three base lines, that see **every fin** by real peers; body cells and fins are never targets. If a fin is *d*, every target sees it; if no fin is *d*, the *d* of each base line is a body cell, the three lie on three different covers, so every cover holds its *d* in a base line and no target is *d*.
  A base line with fewer than three body cells (**sparse**) is allowed by the same proof and is **not** classified as sashimi: no flag, id, key or Finding field; the shared comment is SA2's wording and unchanged.
- **Finding.** `{ technique: 'finnedSwordfish', cells (every base candidate, ascending), placements: [], eliminations (ascending), i18nKey: 'technique.finnedSwordfish.step', i18nArgs: { digit, bases (unit order), covers (unit order), fins (cell order) } }`; the arrays are the call's own. Order: digit, rows then columns, base triples in line order, cover triples in unit order, the first removing pattern wins (the bare vector's second, symmetrical reading is pinned: rows first, a decoy cell ends it).
- **Work (R10), independent limit.** One unit is one tested complete combination of three eligible base lines of one digit and orientation, counted before the rejection checks; all cover choices of the combination are tried inside it (at most 4, 20, 84, 220, 560 and 2,925 trials per unit), so the unit is not a count of cover tests, visibility checks or targets. **`SG.fishSearch.FINNED_SWORDFISH_WORK_LIMIT = 50000`**, its own constant, used by the registered finder and by the seam `SG.fishSearch.finnedSwordfish` (`finnedFish({ size: 3 })`); `FINNED_X_WING_WORK_LIMIT` and the n = 2 finder and seam are unchanged. Local counter, the parent budget at entry and with at most 256 units between checks, a spent limit is `null` with `capHit: 'work'` (a deterministic truncation of the base-combination prefix, never a proof of absence), a witness found before it is complete, a spent budget is nothing found, cancellation and other errors propagate, observer `{ id, work, limit, capHit, retained: null }`.
- **Structural maximum** (`structural-max.txt`), units per call at n = 3: 32 (4×4), 240 (6×6), 1,512 (every 9×9 board), 5,280 (12 digits), 17,920 (16 digits), **128,520 (Sohei, Butterfly) and 255,420 (Samurai, Flower)**: upper bounds, not observations. **Pattern cost** (`pattern-cost.txt`/`.json`, 20 runs): 121 and 129 units (positives), 86, 184 and 338 (negatives), none capped.
- **Observations** (`work-census.json`, `logs/work-census-n3.txt`; finder measurements of one process, not GP or qualification verdicts): **3,508 sampled calls on the agreement corpora, none capped**; the largest work is the Samurai 23,466 units (47 % of the limit; p95 and max 212.353 ms of its 12 seed-4242 calls), the Sohei 8,412 (p95 and max 70.517 ms of its 12 seed-777 calls), 16 digits 4,342 (p95 21.813 ms, max 23.382 ms over 32 calls), classic9 567 (seed 99, 1,200 calls: p95 0.697 ms, max 2.163 ms) and the Flower 567. **Eight constructed calls**, kept apart: the only cap hit is the blank Samurai with every digit open (52,650 combinations, 50,000 tested, 3.781 s), the blank Sohei is 14,688 units (0.948 s), one open digit on the Samurai 5,850 units (0.444 s).
