## 1. The technique (as it stands)

- `uniqueRectangle` (group `wing`, **order 180, base cost 66, weight 900**, unscoped, `assumesUniqueness: true`, all unchanged; 35 techniques, one `uniqueRectangle`) now names four subtypes (`technique.uniqueRectangle.type1.step` to `.type4.step`). The first pass (types 1 and 2, the older code, every rectangle, unbounded, its own `ctx.check()` every 64 rectangles) runs first; only when it finds nothing does the **bounded second pass** run, rectangle by rectangle in the same order, **type 3 across every unit that holds both roofs (ascending index) before type 4 on that rectangle**, the first Finding returned. Preconditions unchanged: `buildRectangles` / `isDeadlyGeometry` (every unit that touches the four cells holds exactly two of them), no cages or inequalities, `assumeUniqueness: false` gives `null`.
- A rectangle qualifies for the second pass when all four cells are open, the AND of the masks is exactly the pair {X, Y}, exactly two corners (the floors) hold exactly the pair and the other two (the roofs) are proper supersets; the extras E are the roofs' candidates outside the pair (one virtual member of a subset, never a fixed extra digit in both roofs).
- **Type 3** (any unit that holds both roofs, partial units included: the proof needs only that its cells differ): *k* other open cells (the real cells, ascending, subsets by size then lexicographic) whose candidates with E are exactly *k* + 1 digits D; every other open cell of the unit loses the digits of D it still has. **No cap on *k* (SA2, R14):** *k* runs from max(1, |E| - 1) to min(eligible cells, digits - 1); a real cell needs its candidates with E to be at most min(eligible, digits - 1) + 1 digits; exact |D| = *k* + 1 at a leaf; the 50,000 limit is the only bound. Finding: `cells` = the four rectangle cells in rectangle order, then the real cells ascending; eliminations by cell then digit; `i18nArgs { roof1, roof2 (labels, lower cell first), digits [X, Y], partDigits D, cells (real-cell labels), unit }`.
- **Type 4** (a complete unit, `ctx.completeUnits[u] === 1`): the digit L of the pair, low to high, whose only holders are the two roofs (a solved holder counts) is the link; the other digit leaves both roofs. Finding: `cells` = the four rectangle cells, eliminations the two roofs, `i18nArgs { roof1, roof2, digits, digit (the removed one), unit }`.
- **Work:** one unit = one tested pool cell as the next real cell (rejections charged) or one tested digit of a link; **50,000 per call** (`SG.wingSearch.UNIQUE_RECTANGLE_WORK_LIMIT`, the seam `SG.wingSearch.uniqueRectangle(model, state, ctx, { workLimit })`), local counter, entry check and at most 256 units between parent-budget checks; a spent limit is `null` with `capHit: 'work'`, a spent parent budget in the second pass is `null` with `capHit: null` (the caller's budget stays spent), cancellation and other errors propagate without a record; observer `{ id: 'uniqueRectangle', work, limit, capHit, retained: null }` only for calls that run the second pass.
- **Text:** the Type 3 last sentence (SA2): EN "{partDigits} are removed from the cells of {unit} outside both {roof1} and {roof2} and the cells {cells}."; VI "Loại {partDigits} khỏi các ô của {unit}, trừ cả {roof1}, {roof2} và các ô {cells}."; the rest of the capacity explanation is as before, in both apps.
