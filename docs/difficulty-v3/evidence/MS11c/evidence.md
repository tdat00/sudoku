## 1. What is implemented

`core/js/engine/tech-wing.js`: `bugPlusOne` (group `wing`, **order 182, base cost 72, weight 920**, unscoped, `assumesUniqueness: true`), registered between `uniqueRectangle` (180) and `forcingChain` (190); the weight is in `generator/js/engine/difficulty.js`. Guard (no work): exactly one open cell T with three candidates and every other open cell with exactly two; a model with no cages, no inequalities and only complete units (`ctx.completeUnits`, one partial unit refuses the whole model); `ctx.assumeUniqueness: false` gives `null` and no record. Then for each candidate X of T in ascending order: with X left out of T, **every unit of the model** (distant units and extra complete constraints included) holds each placed digit in its own solved cell only and every other digit in exactly two cells. The first X that passes is placed (at most one can). A digit X already placed in a unit of T is a stale candidate and never qualifies (SA2's ruling 4: a placement-feasibility guard; an X fixed only outside T's units is not rejected). The Finding: `cells [T]`, `placements [[T, X]]`, `eliminations []`, `i18nKey technique.bugPlusOne.step`, `i18nArgs { cell (index), label ('R5C5'), digit }`. **Proof (R14, in the header of the file, `generator/design.md` and the guides):** the position without X in T has every open cell bivalue and every unplaced digit in exactly two holders per unit; giving every open cell its other candidate is again a solution, so it has none or at least two (**at least two, not exactly two**, SA2's ruling 6); a unique puzzle has one solution, therefore T = X.

Work (R10): one unit is one cell of one unit tallied under one proposed digit (fixed and open cells count), **50,000 per call**, a local counter, an entry check and at most 256 units between checks of the parent budget (`SG.wingSearch.BUG_PLUS_ONE_WORK_LIMIT`; seam `SG.wingSearch.bugPlusOne(model, state, ctx, { workLimit })`); a spent limit is `null` with `capHit: 'work'`, a spent budget `null` with `capHit: null` (the caller's budget stays spent). Observer: **one record on each normal return** whose uniqueness context did not refuse the call (work 0 for an ordinary guard refusal), none on cancellation, an unexpected error or `assumeUniqueness: false`. The shared bounded-pass helper takes the record id as its first argument; the Unique Rectangle passes `'uniqueRectangle'` and keeps its IDs, order, limits and deferred publication (all 435 earlier wing tests pass on the final source).

**The five equivalent mutants** are #16 (a count above two accepted, only fewer refused), #17 (a count below two accepted, only more refused), #18 (a count of three accepted), #19 and #20 (the last or the first digit of a unit not checked). SA2's non-circular argument (ruling 5): in a complete unit of N cells with S fixed cells and P distinct fixed digits (P <= S), with X virtually left out each open cell has two candidates, so the tallied sum is **A = 2N - S** and the required sum **E = 2N - P >= A**. #16 and #18 accept only counts at least their required values, so E >= A forces E = A and equality for every digit; #17 requires every count at most its required value, so each placed digit has exactly one fixed occurrence and no open holder (S = P) and the equal sums force every other count to two; #19 and #20 omit one digit q: if q is unplaced the checked placed counts give S = P and the sum forces q to two, and if q has m fixed occurrences then S - P = m - 1 would need a tally of 2 - m against a real one of at least m, hence m = 1 and q's tally is one. Every version therefore accepts or rejects the same unit before the next, and since the cells, not the digit comparisons, spend the work, the work and the check positions are unchanged too. The explicit equality and all-digit code is kept. `logs/equivalence-search.txt` (850,557 random states that pass the guard on 4 and 6 digits, 517,182 accepted proposals, 0 disagreements for the five variants) only corroborates the argument; it does not prove exhaustiveness. The other 62 mutants cover, per precondition and per limit rule: the open test, one-candidate and four-candidate cells, a second or no trivalue cell, cages, inequalities and each way to mis-read a partial unit, the candidate order, the proposal ending early, the proposed digit left out of the cell or of every cell but one, placed-digit accounting, the tally not cleared, only T's units read, units after the 27th or the first unit not read, solved cells mishandled, the three stale-candidate guard variants, the work count (none, doubled, per unit), the cadence and the entry check, every Finding field (cells, placement, elimination, key, argument shape and order, id), the uniqueness flag, the observer record (not published, wrong id), the limit (one more, the default 50,001 and 49,999, the seam), swallowed cancellation and errors, a spent budget reported as a work limit and the reverse, the base cost, the order, the group, the name key, the scope, the weight (a tie with `uniqueRectangle`, 930) and the scratch tally size.
