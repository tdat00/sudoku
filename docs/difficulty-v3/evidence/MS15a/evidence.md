**The module (what a reviewer needs).** An ALS is *k* open cells (1 to 4) of **one** all-different unit (every entry of `model.units`, complete or partial: a Killer cage, a diagonal, a window, a region, any grid of a multi-grid board) whose candidates are exactly *k* + 1 digits; a bivalue cell is a 1-cell ALS; solved cells are never part of a set; candidates are read as they are. A record is `{ cells, digits, occurrences }` (ascending cells, ascending 1-based digits, the cells holding each digit), one per distinct cell set, in the order **cell count, then the cells lexicographically**. `enumerate(model, state, ctx, work[, limits])` returns `{ records, complete, capHit }` with `capHit` `null` (and `complete` true), `'records'`, `'work'`, or `null` with `complete` false (the parent budget was spent, answered as "nothing found"; cancellation and every other error propagate). The records kept are always a **prefix** of the canonical order: at 4,096 records (the limit refuses the next distinct set, not the 4,096th; a probe that stops at the first further set tells whether one exists) or, when the guard refuses a unit, the sizes finished before the cut (a cut size is dropped whole, because a part of it is not a prefix). **No outcome asserts that another ALS does not exist.** The guard is `work = { limit, used, checkedAt, capped }` from `createWork([limit])` (50,000), spent with `spend(work, ctx)` (true: one unit; false: the limit is spent, `capped` set, nothing charged), never reset by `enumerate`; the parent budget is checked at entry and before the unit that would carry the gap since the last check over 256. One unit is one tested extension (a cell tried as the next member of a combination, whether the union bound then rejects it or not); sizes are searched one after the other and unit by unit; a set that two units share is tested in both and only its record is deduplicated. It publishes **no** `onPatternWork` record.

**What is independent and what is not (SA1 finding 2).** The **record listing** (`oracle`) is independent: it is written from `model.units`, the state and sorted digit lists only (every subset of the open cells of each unit, the union of their digits, a table by the cells): no mask, no search order, no pruning of the engine.
