- **Proof (capacity).** Four distinct open cells of one to four candidates whose candidates together are exactly four digits. A digit is *restricted* when every pair of its occurrences in the four cells are peers (`model.peers`: row, column, box, region, diagonal, window, overlapped box or cage; a digit that occurs in one cell is restricted) and *non-restricted* when two of its occurrences are not; exactly one digit z is non-restricted. Every cell takes one of the four digits; the three restricted digits are each taken by at most one cell, so by at most three cells in all, so **at least one quartet cell takes z**; an open cell outside the quartet that sees **every** quartet cell holding z cannot hold z. Never a target: a quartet cell (it does not see itself). No pivot, no connected or unit shape, no two-candidate minimum (SA2, Q2). Verified by an exhaustive capacity check in the suite (`fillings`).
