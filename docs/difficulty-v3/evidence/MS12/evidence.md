## 1. What is implemented

`core/js/engine/tech-chain.js`: `xyChain` (group `chain`, **order 186, base cost 82, weight 1000**, unscoped, `assumesUniqueness: false`), registered between `bugPlusOne` (182) and `forcingChain` (190); the weight is in `generator/js/engine/difficulty.js`. It is a **restricted-edge mode of the shared forcing search**: `EDGE_FILTERS` (a frozen record of four filters: unit-conjugate strong links, same-cell weak links, the same-digit end test and the polarity proofs) is selected by the new seam option `edges: 'general' | 'restricted'` (any other value is `solver.invalidOption`) and threaded through the builder of the strong links, the weak-link collector, the visit, the root scan and the Finding. In the restricted mode a strong link is only the two candidates of a **bivalue cell**, a weak link only the same digit in two distinct cells that see each other (`model.peers`, partial units included), a search starts only from a **false** literal and may end only at a **true literal of the same digit in a higher cell**, so a proof has 3, 5 to 11 edges and **establishes that at least one of the two cells holds the digit** (every candidate of the digit that sees both cells is eliminated; nothing is placed, no contradiction is searched). The restricted graph does not consult the contents of `ctx.completeUnits` or use unit-conjugate strong links (SA2's wording correction, applied in the header of the file, both designs and the guides). A path may visit a cell twice; the shortest proof of the whole scan is kept (ties: the lowest start node, then the breadth-first order) within the window 1 to 12 edges, and the caller's `maxChainLength` can only narrow it; no seam override can widen the registered finder. The registered `forcingChain`, `forcingChainLong` and `nishio` never read the option.

Finding: `cells` (the distinct cells of the chain, ascending), `placements []`, `eliminations` ascending, `i18nKey technique.xyChain.step` (no kind suffix), `i18nArgs { digit, kind: 'endpoints', length (edges), chains: [one chain of [cell, digit, truth] in traversal order] }`. Work (R10): one unit is one **visited edge** (the proof edge, revisits and edges to terminal non-bivalue candidates included), **50,000 per call** (`NEW_FINDER_WORK_LIMIT`, unchanged), the entry check and at most 256 units between checks of the parent budget; a spent limit is `null` with `capHit: 'work'` and **never a partial minimum**, a spent budget `null` with `capHit: null`; cancellation and every other error propagate; the observer gets one record on each normal return and none otherwise.

Priority: `xyChain` (186) acts before `forcingChain` (190): "shortest" applies within the restricted search, not across ids (SA2's ruling 6; on the shared fixture "contradiction chain of 4 links" a genuine 7-edge XY proof exists as well and the registry reaches it first). No unrestricted proof is relabelled.

**The equivalent mutant (#5): a proof may end in a lower cell than its start (the lower-end rule dropped).** SA2's argument (ruling 5): **reverse the path and complement every truth bit** (plain reversal is not enough). A strong edge is the pair of candidates of one bivalue cell and a weak edge the same digit in two peer cells, both symmetric, so the complemented reverse of a path from "s is false" to "t is true" is a path from "t is false" to "s is true" over restricted edges, of the same length L, with the same digit, the same two cells and therefore the same target set (it depends only on the ends). Suppose the dropped rule newly permitted a match of length L from a higher start to a lower end. The complemented reverse is a lower-start proof whose false literal is an eligible root (it has a strong edge) scanned earlier (ascending root order); its search, limited to the best length so far minus one, finds that proof or another of length at most L, or the best length is already at most L; an exhaustion of the work limit would have ended the whole call before, with `null`. After that root the best length is at most L, so the later search is limited to a length below L and can neither find nor select the match, for every such match: the searches visit exactly the same edges in the same order, so **no new Finding and no change in the charged work**. The added branch can only add intersection checks that no counter meters, so **identical latency is not claimed**. The argument depends on the symmetric edges, endpoint-only targets, eligible roots, ascending root order and the pruning by the best length, and does not transfer to a different graph or search; the guard stays. SA2's 420 finite paired comparisons of Findings and work records matched and corroborate it; they do not replace it. The other 42 mutants cover: the restricted edges (a conjugate pair of a unit as a strong link, two candidates of one cell as a weak link, the two polarities searched, ends of different digits, the end test skipped inside the search, a proof ending in the start cell, a "true" start, a strong link from a cell with more than two candidates, an elimination rule that sees one end, a solved cell as a weak neighbour), the scan (the last proof kept, descending starts), the window (11, 13, the caller's limit not narrowing or widening, a minimum of 5), the limit (50,001, none, off by one, every edge counted twice, the budget checked early or 257 units late, no entry check, swallowed cancellation and errors, a record published for an error, a spent budget reported as a work cap), the Finding (cells in chain order, descending eliminations, the general key, the length in literals, the kind forced, the digit of the second literal, the far anchor) and the registration (order 191, base cost 80, the wing group, uniqueness, a variant scope, the weights 1050, 1100 and 900).
