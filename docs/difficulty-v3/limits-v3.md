# Difficulty v3: the frozen limit record

**Status.** Frozen with rating/cache version **3** (MS17b), as to **section 1 only** (see section 5). This file records the production limits and policies of the MS17b **dispatch inventory**, as they stand in the source at the implementation base `5b94904` (MS17a) plus MS17b's two version edits (`RATING_VERSION` and the cache `SCHEMA_VERSION`, no limit touched): the new-pattern finders and the grouped-forcing policy (sections 1 and 2) and, recorded separately, the generation and window policies (section 3). It is **not a census of every numeric constant of the engine**: the older technique forms (for example `MAX_FISH` in `tech-fish.js`, `MAX_SIZE` and `MAX_UNIT` in `tech-subset.js`, the `MAX_*` constants of `tech-cage.js`), the guided-dig parameters of `digger.js` (`GUIDED_*`) and `DEFAULT_MAX_RESTARTS` in `full-grid.js` are outside this record. It selects nothing: each value below was selected, or retained, by the task named in its row, and the evidence of that decision is the cited file under `docs/difficulty-v3/evidence/`. A later change of a value in **section 1** needs a **new rating version**; MS18 may not tune them. **Section 3 is not frozen by the version**: it is recorded separately, MS18b re-checks R5 and C, and a changed winner goes to the owner, who keeps the value or inserts a change task (check-list GS cap and GS window rows). The record is a versioned policy, not a lock on the code: the diagnostic overrides that the owned MS18b comparisons need (`SG.chainSearch.SHORT_MAX`, `SG.digger.SHALLOW_SEARCH_RATINGS`, the seam options of the finders, the recorder timeouts and command ceilings) stay settable and are not selected production pattern bounds.

**How to read a row.** *Counter* is what one unit of work is and where the counter is kept; *stop* is what the finder does when the counter is spent. For every bounded finder below the counter is local to the call, the parent budget is checked at entry and before the unit that would carry the work since the last check over **256** units, a spent limit ends the search at a deterministic prefix with no Finding from the unfinished part (a lost deduction, never an invented one: it proves no absence), a Finding made before the limit is spent is complete, a spent parent budget is "nothing found" for the caller (which checks its own budget again), and cancellation and every other error propagate. The observer `ctx.options.onPatternWork(record)` reports `{ id, work, limit, capHit, retained }` and never selects a result.

**What the evidence files are.** The paths and the section designations are those of the MS17b dispatch inventory: every file exists and every numbered section that is named (MS11b, MS11c and MS12 section 8; MS13c section 5; MS14b section 6; MS15b section 5; MS16 section 5) exists, while sub-labels such as "C4" are as the inventory cites them. MS17b compiled this record from the source constants and those files; it ran **no new census, candidate-bound trial, counter redefinition or finder change**, and it does not re-derive the measurements cited from the checklist's S-AV items.

### Not "every call has 50,000"

- **Unique Rectangle.** The legacy first pass (types 1 and 2) has **no new local work cap**. The second pass and the hidden third pass have **independent** 50,000 counters. A second-pass local cap can continue into the hidden pass; a parent stop cannot. They are not one 50,000 whole-call allowance.
- **Law of Leftovers** has two kinds of bound (the 2 / 4 selection sizes and the 50,000 whole-call work) and **ALS** has three (cells, records, work): they are different counters.
- **Sue de Coq** has a separate guard; it neither shares nor renews the ALS-XZ guard. A spent shared guard is not renewed.
- **The seam default.** `DEFAULT_WORK_LIMIT = 50000` of the chain seam is the default of the seam only, not a new local limit on the legacy unbounded paths; every registered caller passes its policy explicitly.
- **Fixed technique forms are not tunable work bounds.** The windows below are part of what a technique *is* in rating v3.

## 2. Grouped forcing: S-OD11 (owner decision, Discord, 2026-11-17 04:30 +07:00, "GC-01: chọn A.")

Short forcing first makes the bounded grouped attempt (4,096 groups, 50,000 units for construction and search together). **Only a local work/node cap**, with the parent still available, allows the complete singleton fallback with **no local work cap**, the same parent budget and window; the minimum then belongs to the singleton domain. Long forcing has no fallback and no second allowance: an incomplete graph or minimum yields no Finding. There is no fallback for parent exhaustion, errors, cancellation, completed negatives or window rejection. IR54 and the phase / whole-call observer distinctions are preserved, and both phases and their total cost are recorded by the observer. R10's once-only new-limit rule is not permission to change this policy.

## 5. What freezing means here

- **Frozen with the version:** the numeric values and meanings of the **new-pattern limits of section 1**, as a versioned record. A change of one of them needs a new rating version and a new record.
- **Recorded separately, not frozen by the version:** the generation and window policies of **section 3** (the R9 chain cutoff C, the R5 shallow allowance, the size-dependent attempts 8 / 4 / 2, the existing dig bounds and the Greater-Than sign pass). They are retained values that MS18b re-checked (R5 and C) on 2026-11-18: both selections were reconfirmed (256 and C = 4, `evidence/MS18b/`), so no owner decision followed; the values remain recorded separately and are not frozen by the version.
- **Not frozen in code:** the diagnostic overrides and seams listed in the status paragraph; no `Object.freeze` was added to a mutable diagnostic namespace.
- **Not decided here:** availability, cost, qualification and offers. They stay with MS18c, MS18d and MS18e; a limit is a cap on a search, not a measurement of how often the finder is needed.
