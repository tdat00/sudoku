# The final matched before/after record (MS18a)

This is the final matched comparison of the complete difficulty-v3 candidate (MS17b, `57c1b10`) with the actual MS02
baseline, on the same 17 cells and the same 204 request keys. The commands, ledgers, transcripts, readiness records, lock
log, helper hashes and the generated tables are in [evidence/MS18a/](evidence/MS18a/evidence.md); every figure below is
computed from raw rows by the task-owned reporter (`comparison-tables.md`, `comparison.json`), never from an estimate.

**Read this first.** All 204 requests ran and all 204 outcomes are retained; no request errored, and the soak's diagnostic
checks passed in all 32 commands. **Every timing here is "shared-slot, CLI-host-active; not strict-quiet-machine timing"**
(MS18a-Q1); the old MS02 times were taken while the Pentomino repository ran quick tests. Timings are descriptive, not a GQ
or GP result and not a bound for anything. Old score-only hits are shown in their own column and are never counted as
required exactness.

**Baseline Hell 1 was vacuous**: its tier was empty (`required: true` without a solve, adjusted position undefined), so
its 12 old qualified hits are the 12 in-band ones and not a measured requiredness.

## How the commands were admitted (MS18a-Q1-HOST, MS18a-Q2-ADMISSION)

Every command ran under the confirmed shared slot, the named policy `MS18a-Q1-host-active` and the label above, one at a
time (jobs 1, at least 15 s apart, each under `Invoke-Dv3Node`, no foreign timing lock ever seen). The raw strict screen
(mean at most 0.5 and max at most 1.0 busy cores of 16) is recorded unchanged: it **failed in 26 of the 34 admission
attempts** (the two holds and 24 of the 32 executed commands; the other 8 commands passed it). Admission was decided
separately by the named policy, which requires the verified Copilot host instance (PID 1968, creation time checked)
and no other process at 0.5 CPU-s per second or more over the
snapshot window; no CPU was subtracted and no failed screen was turned into a pass. The raw CPU does not establish
host-only attribution. **Two entries were held once**, each by a single `svchost.exe` consumer (`killer-expert-9-3`:
PID 10948, 0.66 per second; `jigsaw9-hard`: PID 3640, 0.59 per second), payload unstarted, and were executed at their
second admission attempt at least 60 s after the release (Q2); no entry needed a third and no payload ran twice. Per-
command rows, both attempts of each held entry and the transcripts are in `comparison-tables.md` section 11.

## What this does not show

- It is twelve requests per cell: a cell's count is of 12, with no sampling statement. Timings are single shared-machine
  observations; there was no repeat and no strict-quiet measurement, so no GQ or GP conclusion follows.
- The new counts for 170 of the 204 rows rest on the derived certificate; only 34 rows were independently referenced.
- It does not explain why a cell moved. The solver, cutoff, attempt policy and target changed between the two sources.
- It does not decide the three cells without an exact puzzle (killer hard, killer expert, classic9 hell1): they are
  reported as measured and routed to the owners below; no limit, weight or tolerance was changed.
